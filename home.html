About — Index 002
AR / PORTRAIT FIG. 02.1

Precision is a design decision, not an accident.

I'm a design engineer working across product design and front-end development. Over the past six years I've turned early ideas into interfaces that feel considered, fast, and unmistakably deliberate — moving fluidly between Figma and code so nothing gets lost in translation.

My focus is the space where systems thinking meets craft: building design systems that scale, motion that clarifies rather than decorates, and front-end architecture that holds up as projects grow. I care about the details most people never consciously notice — and that most people still feel.
